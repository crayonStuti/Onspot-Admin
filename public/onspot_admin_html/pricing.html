Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use On S.P.O.T. for free?
Yes. The Free Explorer plan provides access to essential features so you can experience the platform before upgrading.
Can I change my plan later?
Absolutely. As your needs evolve, you can upgrade or adjust your subscription at any time.
Is the Family Plan limited to one household?
Family plans are designed for individuals who regularly plan and participate in outdoor activities together. Specific account limits can be outlined within the subscription details.
Will my saved locations and records transfer if I upgrade?
Yes. Your information remains connected to your account, making transitions between plans simple and seamless.
