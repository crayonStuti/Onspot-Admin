Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Whether you're planning your first adventure with On S.P.O.T. or exploring the benefits of a premium membership, we're here to help. Browse our most commonly asked questions below to learn more about the platform and how it can enhance your outdoor experiences.

General Questions

What is On S.P.O.T.?
On S.P.O.T. is an outdoor companion app designed to help hunters and anglers organize, plan, and track their adventures. From saving favorite locations and accessing helpful resources to documenting trips and building a personal outdoor history, On S.P.O.T. keeps everything in one convenient place.
Who is On S.P.O.T. designed for?
On S.P.O.T. is built for anyone who enjoys spending time outdoors, including:
  • Hunters
  • Anglers
  • Outdoor explorers
  • Families who adventure together
  • Beginners learning the ropes
  • Experienced outdoorsmen and women looking to stay organized
Is On S.P.O.T. available nationwide?
Yes. On S.P.O.T. is designed to support outdoor enthusiasts across the United States, providing resources that help users prepare for adventures wherever they choose to explore.

Account & Membership Questions

Do I need an account to use On S.P.O.T.?
Yes. Creating an account allows you to save locations, track activities, access personalized features, and securely store your outdoor records.
Is there a free version available?
Absolutely. On S.P.O.T. offers a free option so users can explore the platform and experience its core functionality before deciding whether to upgrade.
Is there a family membership option?
Yes. Family plans allow multiple users to enjoy On S.P.O.T. together, making it easier to plan adventures, organize locations, and create lasting memories.
Can I upgrade my membership later?
Yes. You can upgrade your plan at any time to unlock additional features and expand your outdoor toolkit.
Will I lose my saved information if I switch plans?
No. Your saved records remain associated with your account, making upgrades and plan changes seamless.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. Subscription management is simple, allowing you to make changes based on your needs and lifestyle.

Features & Functionality

What can I do with the mapping features?
On S.P.O.T.'s mapping tools help you organize your adventures by allowing you to:
  • Save favorite hunting and fishing locations
  • Mark places you'd like to revisit
  • Plan future outings
  • Keep important locations accessible whenever you need them
Can I track my hunting and fishing trips?
Yes. On S.P.O.T. helps you document your experiences so you can build a personalized record of your outdoor activities. You can log details such as:
  • Dates of outings
  • Locations visited
  • Notes and observations
  • Personal achievements and milestones
Outdoor enthusiasts increasingly value the ability to record trip details and revisit past experiences to improve future outings.
Can I access hunting and fishing regulations through the app?
On S.P.O.T. provides convenient access to licensing and regulation resources designed to help you prepare responsibly before heading outdoors. Because regulations can change, users should always verify official requirements with the appropriate state agency before participating in hunting or fishing activities.
Can family members share saved locations?
Depending on your selected plan, shared access features may allow family members to collaborate on trip planning and outdoor experiences.

Technical Questions

Is On S.P.O.T. available on iPhone and Android devices?
On S.P.O.T. is designed to provide a mobile-friendly experience for outdoor enthusiasts. Specific device availability can be listed alongside download options on the app's download page.
Does On S.P.O.T. work in areas with limited service?
Certain features may require an internet connection, while others may remain accessible depending on how the platform is configured. Users should review feature specifications before relying on the app in remote areas.
How do I reset my password?
If you've forgotten your password, simply select the "Forgot Password" option on the login screen and follow the instructions sent to your email address.
Who should I contact if I need help?
Our support team is here to assist you. If you experience technical issues or have questions about your account, visit our Contact Us page for assistance.

Privacy & Security Questions

Is my information secure?
Protecting your personal information is important to us. On S.P.O.T. is committed to maintaining appropriate safeguards to help protect user data and account information.
Are my saved locations visible to other users?
No. Your personal records and saved locations remain private unless you choose to share information through features specifically designed for collaboration.

Getting Started

How do I begin using On S.P.O.T.?
Getting started is easy:
  1. Download the app.
  2. Create your account.
  3. Explore maps and outdoor resources.
  4. Save your favorite locations.
  5. Start documenting your adventures.
Do I need to be an experienced hunter or angler to use the app?
Not at all. On S.P.O.T. was designed for outdoor enthusiasts of every experience level. Whether you're heading out for your very first fishing trip or have decades of stories to tell, the platform provides tools that are easy to understand and use.
