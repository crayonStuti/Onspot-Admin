FAQs

What do I get with the Free Plan?
You can explore basic hunting and fishing spots, track your licenses manually, and access community tips.
Is there an annual discount for Premium?
Yes — choose the annual plan and save 25% versus the monthly price.
How is the Premium Plan different?
Premium unlocks unlimited verified spots, offline maps, weather forecasts, automatic license sync, the member social portal, and exclusive partner discounts.
Can I switch from Free to Premium anytime?
Absolutely. Upgrade or cancel any time from your profile — your saved spots and history stay with you.
Do I need internet to use the app outdoors?
Premium offers downloadable offline maps so you can navigate and log spots without a signal.
